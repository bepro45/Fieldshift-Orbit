Not yet integrated

The challenge lists more datasets than this prototype ingests. Not used here: ECOSTRESS, GRACE/GRACE-FO, GEDI, Sentinel-2, NLDAS/GLDAS, OpenET, and US-only soil/crop layers (SSURGO, CDL, PRISM, NASS) — the pilot site is in Uganda. Soil comes from SoilGrids instead of Web Soil Survey. Adding a source is one new module in the backend.

Objectives coverage

  • 3+ strategies compared incl. your existing rotation
  • Why suggested · evidence · benefits · gaps · risks on every card
  • Farmer priorities, food-vs-market and residue inputs
  • Rainfall & temperature what-if sliders, four climate scenarios
  • Bare-field periods, terrain, soil uncertainty, resolution ladder
  • Explains ET₀, NDVI, soil moisture, dry spells, satellite vs ground
06 · Limits & human judgment

A supportive tool — not a verdict.

Support, not replace.Scores rank options under stated assumptions; they are not yield or profit forecasts. Farmers, extension officers and agronomists should review every suggestion before acting.
